Community Living Supports in Redding, California

Community Living Supports (CLS) help a person with a developmental disability take part in everyday life, at home and around town, with a trained ACP staff member by their side.

Questions? Call (530) 781-6701 or send us a request

What are Community Living Supports?

Help with everyday life, out in the community

Community Living Supports is the name of a service in California's Self-Determination Program (SDP). It pays for support that helps a person build daily living skills and join in community life.

CLS is nonmedical help with daily tasks and community activities the person chooses. Support might include preparing for a meal, visiting a library, or taking part in an approved activity, with our staff member helping them practice skills along the way.

CLS is a separate program from respite and from clinical treatment. Before scheduling, we review mobility and personal-care arrangements.

Every activity follows the person's interests and goals, and must be allowed by their Individual Program Plan (IPP) and SDP spending plan.

Our approach

Taking part in community life, at a comfortable pace

We help people with developmental disabilities take part in their community, build connections, and work toward more everyday independence. Support follows each person's interests, preferences, and goals.

From recreational outings to practicing everyday routines, we give individual support that encourages real participation at a pace that feels comfortable. We work with individuals, families, and support teams so support stays consistent and trauma-informed beyond the home. Trauma-informed means we keep in mind that hard past experiences can shape how a person reacts, so we focus on safety, trust, and choice.

What we focus on

  • Community participationSupport with chosen recreational activities, local outings, and social opportunities.
  • Everyday skillsChances to practice communication, decision-making, and familiar community routines.
  • Individual choiceActivities shaped by the person's interests, comfort, and personal goals.
  • Consistent supportWorking with families and caregivers so the same strategies are used in every setting.
What's included

What we do, and our care limits

We review every person's needs before services start. If our service isn't the right fit, we'll tell you clearly and point you toward other options when we can.

What CLS can include

  • Preparing for a meal and other everyday tasks
  • Visiting the library, parks, trails, and recreation activities
  • Shopping and errands, including practice with choosing items and paying
  • Joining local events and social activities
  • Practicing communication and making choices
  • Building routines that help with independence

Care limits: what we can't do

  • Activities not allowed by the person's IPP and SDP spending plan
  • Overnight care
  • Choosing, measuring, or giving medicine, or any nursing care
  • Lifting, or moving someone by hand or with a lift machine
  • Driving participants. ACP staff don't drive participants to activities.
  • Emergency response. If someone is in danger or needs medical help, call 911.
Where we serve

Service area

Redding, CA, and eligible locations in Shasta and Tehama counties within about a 30-minute drive of Redding. Coverage depends on the exact location and travel time. Send us your address or general area and we will confirm.

Outings start and end at a place we agree on with your family.

Whether we can start depends on your location, your loved one's needs, payment approval, and which trained staff are available.

How to get a referral

  1. Contact us. Call (530) 781-6701 or email acp.info@arcioniscarepartners.com. Tell us your name, how to reach you, your general area, and that you're interested in Community Living Supports.
  2. Talk about goals. We'll talk about what your loved one enjoys, what skills they're working on, mobility and personal-care needs, and the days and times you want.
  3. Add CLS to your SDP spending plan. Ask your service coordinator or Independent Facilitator (IF) to add ACP for Community Living Supports. We'll give your team the service details they need, and they'll connect it to an IPP goal.
  4. Finish approval and paperwork. Your regional center approves (certifies) the spending plan. Then we complete setup with your Financial Management Services (FMS) company and sign a service agreement.
  5. Start outings. We hold an intake meeting and begin once authorization and a trained staff member are in place. We check in with your family and adjust as goals change.
Questions

Common questions

More answers about funding, the Self-Determination Program, and getting started are in our full FAQ.

Do I need to be in SDP to get CLS?

Community Living Supports is set up through an SDP spending plan. If you use traditional regional center services, ask your service coordinator about options and contact us about current availability. Our FAQ explains how to start SDP.

How is CLS different from respite?

Respite is mainly a break for the caregiver. CLS is focused on the person: building skills and taking part in the community. It is a separate program from respite and clinical treatment.

Can activities change over time?

Yes. Tell us and your planning team when interests or goals change. Bigger changes may need an update to the SDP spending plan.
