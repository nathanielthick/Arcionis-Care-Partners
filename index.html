What we do

Crisis Intervention

We prioritize long-term crisis stability and safety over temporary fixes. Recognizing that acute crises often stem from unaddressed trauma and underlying stress, our Crisis Intervention services combine non-coercive, trauma-informed principles to de-escalate immediate distress, minimize physical intervention, and build lasting security.

By integrating PCMA’s Everyday BehaviorTools alongside Ukeru Systems training, our Crisis Intervention team emphasizes the least-restrictive environment possible—focusing on comfort, communication, and safety during high-stress moments.

Our Crisis Intervention strategy features a customized plan consisting of:

  • Trauma-Informed Risk Assessments: Utilizing Ukeru’s brain-based research to identify environmental triggers, trauma history, and safety risks to prevent escalation before it starts.
  • Proactive Skill Building (PCMA Everyday BehaviorTools): Implementing practical and non-reactive strategies to maintain emotional balance and promote self-regulation.
  • Least-Restrictive De-Escalation Techniques: Leveraging Ukeru’s soft-equipment blocking techniques to safely absorb and divert physical energy, protecting everyone involved while minimizing the need for restrictive measures.
  • Proactive Staff & Support Training: Equipping care teams with relationship-focused communication skills and early intervention tools to foster predictability, trust, and safety.
  • Community Integration & Continuums of Care: Establishing continuous, trauma-sensitive support networks that ensure smooth transitions and maintain long-term stability in the community.

Respite

We prioritize sustainable, long-term stability by providing high-quality, proactive non-crisis respite care. Recognizing that routine relief is essential for preventing caregiver burnout and maintaining a healthy home environment, our respite services focus on creating a safe, low-stress space where individuals feel comfortable, understood, and supported.

By integrating PCMA’s Everyday BehaviorTools alongside Ukeru Systems principles, our trained staff seamlessly maintain your loved one's routines in a trauma-informed, least-restrictive setting—ensuring continuity of care while caregivers take a well-deserved break.

Our planned respite strategy features a customized support plan consisting of:

  • Routine & Environment Assessments: Understanding unique preferences, personal triggers, and comfort needs to ensure a smooth transition and a predictable, stress-free respite stay.
  • Proactive Engagement (PCMA Everyday BehaviorTools): Utilizing gentle, positive strategies to maintain emotional balance, build trust, and keep daily routines engaging.
  • Comfort-Focused Safety Measures: Applying Ukeru’s trauma-informed, least-restrictive approach to foster a comforting atmosphere that prioritizes physical and emotional well-being.
  • Caregiver & Staff Alignment: Collaborating closely with families to mirror everyday support strategies, ensuring continuity, comfort, and peace of mind for everyone involved.
  • Community & Recreational Outings: Offering structured, low-stress community connection opportunities designed to foster positive experiences and support overall well-being.
