Our core service

Community Support Services in Redding, CA

Arcionis Care Partners helps individuals with developmental disabilities participate in their community, build connections, and work toward greater everyday independence. Our community support services in Redding, California, follow each person’s interests, preferences, and goals.

From recreational outings to practicing everyday routines, we provide individualized support that encourages meaningful participation at a comfortable pace. We collaborate with individuals, families, and support teams to create consistent, trauma-informed support beyond the home.

Our community support services focus on:

  • Community participation: Support with chosen recreational activities, local outings, and social opportunities.
  • Everyday skills: Opportunities to practice communication, decision-making, and familiar community routines.
  • Individual choice: Activities shaped by the person’s interests, comfort, and personal goals.
  • Consistent support: Coordination with families and caregivers to maintain familiar strategies across settings.

In-Home Respite Care in Redding, CA

Family caregivers need dependable opportunities to rest, recharge, and manage everyday responsibilities. Arcionis Care Partners provides planned in-home respite care in Redding, California, while supporting your loved one’s familiar routines, preferences, and activities.

Our trauma-informed approach emphasizes dignity, comfort, and positive relationships. We work with families to understand communication styles, support needs, and daily routines before services begin.

Our respite services focus on:

  • Caregiver relief: Scheduled support that gives family caregivers time for themselves.
  • Familiar routines: Continuity with the activities and support strategies your loved one knows.
  • Meaningful engagement: Activities tailored to individual interests and preferences.
  • Family collaboration: Clear communication about expectations, needs, and ongoing support.

Crisis Intervention & Stabilization Support in Redding, CA

Arcionis Care Partners provides trauma-informed crisis intervention and stabilization support for individuals with developmental disabilities in Redding, California. With oversight from our Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA), we work with individuals, families, and support teams to understand distress and develop individualized support strategies.

Our approach incorporates PCMA BehaviorTools and Ukeru principles, emphasizing prevention, communication, de-escalation, and the least-restrictive support appropriate to the individual.

Our crisis support services focus on:

  • Prevention planning: Identifying triggers, environmental stressors, and early signs of distress.
  • Individualized strategies: Support guided by the person’s needs and clinical plan.
  • De-escalation: Responses that prioritize comfort, dignity, and safety.
  • Caregiver training: Practical tools to help families and support teams respond consistently.
