What we do

Community supports, respite care, and scheduled behavioral services for individuals with developmental disabilities

Our services share a trauma-informed, least-restrictive approach, built around each person's interests, routines, and goals. All three programs serve people age 5 and older, with no upper age limit.

Community Supports

One-on-one, nonmedical help with daily routines and community activities the person chooses, at home and around Redding.

In-Home Respite

Planned breaks for family caregivers, with the behavioral support your loved one's plan calls for, at home.

Scheduled Behavioral Services

Getting started

How it works

Getting started is simple. Questions about referrals, the Self-Determination Program, or scheduling? Read our FAQ.

01

Reach out

Call us or send a message. Tell us a little about your loved one and the kind of support you're looking for.

02

Get to know each other

We learn about routines, preferences, triggers, and goals, talk through which program fits best, and help you connect with your Far Northern Regional Center (FNRC) service coordinator for a referral.

03

Support begins

Your provider follows a customized plan, and we stay in close contact with your family and support team as needs change.

Service area

Serving Redding and Nearby Communities

We serve Redding, CA, and eligible locations in Shasta and Tehama counties within about a 30-minute drive of Redding. Contact Arcionis Care Partners to discuss your location, support needs, schedule, and the referral and authorization steps.
