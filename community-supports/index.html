Community Supports in Redding, California

Our community supports pair individuals with developmental disabilities with a trained Arcionis provider for planned outings and activities around Redding. Your loved one builds connection, confidence, and independence, and you get a well-deserved break.

Questions about community supports? Call (530) 781-6701

What are community supports?

Support that happens out in the community, not just at home

Community supports are services where a trained provider goes out into the community with an individual with a developmental disability. Together they take part in planned, low-stress activities that match the person's interests, routines, and goals.

Every outing is guided by a customized support plan built with your family, and by the same trauma-informed, least-restrictive approach we use in all of our services, grounded in PCMA's Everyday BehaviorTools and Ukeru Systems principles.

Community supports help with

  • Social connection and making the most of time in the community
  • Communication and everyday living skills, practiced in real places
  • Confidence and independence, one outing at a time
  • Predictable, positive experiences built around comfort and safety
  • Regular breaks for parents and caregivers
What it looks like

Community outings around Redding

Every plan is different. Activities are chosen around what your loved one enjoys and what they're working toward. Here are some of the things community supports can include.

Fresh air, walks, and time outdoors at local parks and trails.

Recreation

Hobbies, games, and recreational activities that build positive experiences.

Shopping & errands

Social skills

Opportunities to practice communication and connect with others.

Everyday independence

Building routines and confidence that carry over into daily life.

FAQ

Community supports questions

Don't see your question here? Call us at (530) 781-6701 or send us a message.

What are community supports?

Community supports are services where a trained provider accompanies an individual with a developmental disability into the community for planned activities. The goal is to build social connection, everyday living skills, confidence, and independence, while giving caregivers a break.

Who are community supports for?

Our community supports are for individuals with developmental disabilities in and around Redding, California who enjoy getting out and trying new things, and for the families and caregivers who support them.

What's the difference between community supports and home respite?

Home respite happens in your home, where a provider cares for your loved one in a familiar setting. Community supports take place out in the community through planned outings and activities. Many families use a mix of both.

How do you keep outings safe?

Every outing follows a customized support plan. Our providers use PCMA's Everyday BehaviorTools and Ukeru Systems principles, a trauma-informed, least-restrictive approach that focuses on comfort, communication, and safety.

Do you work with regional centers?

Yes. We work closely with families, regional centers, independent facilitators, and each person's support team to make sure services actually fit the individual.

How do we get started?

Call us at (530) 781-6701 or send a message through our contact page. We'll learn about your loved one and talk through whether community supports, home respite, or a combination is the best fit.
