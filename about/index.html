Our mission

What is our mission?

Arcionis Care Partners exists to provide the highest quality care possible to individuals with developmental disabilities. We are committed to improving the lives of our clients and their families through compassionate, personalized care. Our team of dedicated professionals works tirelessly to ensure that every individual receives the support they need to thrive in their community.

What guides our work

01

Dignity & respect

Every person is treated with compassion and supported to live a meaningful, independent life.

02

Trauma-informed care

We look beneath behavior to the stress and history that shape it.

03

Least-restrictive support

Comfort, communication, and safety come before physical intervention.

04

Partnership with families

We work alongside families and support teams so care stays consistent.
