The people behind Arcionis.

We prioritize relationship-first support and work toward consistent, high-quality continuity of care.

How our team works with you

  • We aim to keep the same providers with each family when staffing allows
  • Close collaboration with families, regional centers, independent facilitators, and each person's support team
  • Staff trained in PCMA Everyday BehaviorTools and Ukeru Systems
  • Locally based in Redding, California

Angela McPherson

Agency Director and Compliance Officer

Angie oversees staffing, training, scheduling, safety processes, and service readiness. She retains operational approval for accepting assignments and provides a clear point of accountability when arrangements need attention.

Michael Buckner

Chief Program Officer and Chief Marketing Officer

Michael helps families and referral partners understand Arcionis Care Partners' programs and next steps. Under the Agency Director's oversight, he coordinates referral communications, program materials, and training records. Clinical questions go to our Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA).

Noah Stutesman

Chief Financial Officer

Noah oversees financial records, payroll, authorization reconciliation, and billing support. He works with operations to keep financial processes connected to the services actually authorized and delivered.

Meghan Fry

BCBA and Clinical Supervisor

As our Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA), Meghan directs clinical assessment and treatment in the behavior intervention program, reviews intervention plans, and supervises assigned clinical work.
